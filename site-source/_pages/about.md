I am a PhD student at [MIT IDSS](https://idss.mit.edu/) with [Prof. Jinhua Zhao](https://zhaojinhua.com/). I also work with [Prof. Paul Liang](https://pliang279.github.io/).

Before relocating to Cambridge, I spent two years in Hong Kong and received my M.Phil in Operations Research from [the Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/) under [Prof. Anthony Man-Cho So](https://www1.se.cuhk.edu.hk/~manchoso/) and [Prof. Viet Anh Nguyen](https://www.vietanhnguyen.net/). I obtained my bachelor’s degree in Mathematics (2019-2023) from Tongji University.

I now study how **LLMs acquire and internalize knowledge as they improve themselves**.

<!-- research-background -->

I started my research career in 2022, studying optimization theory and game theory for complex decision-making systems ([Goods markets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07759), [chores markets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04036)). Starting in 2024, motivated by the development of GenAI, I have explored the two-way interaction between optimization and LLMs: applying optimization methods to improve LLM training and inference ([FLORAIN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04043), [GETS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.09570)), and developing LLMs that can better perform optimization modeling ([AlphaOPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18428)) and algorithm design ([FrontierOR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25246)). After joining MIT in 2025, I have become increasingly interested in the broader methodologies for improving LLMs themselves. I see LLMs as optimizers that can propose solutions, learn from feedback, and refine the approaches they generate ([CORAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01658)). My current research explores how test-time training can strengthen this self-improvement process, allowing models to improve their capabilities as they work on a problem ([Guidance-TTT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06269)). I also contribute to [REEF](https://github.com/Human-Agent-Society/reef), an open-source infrastructure project for continual learning.
