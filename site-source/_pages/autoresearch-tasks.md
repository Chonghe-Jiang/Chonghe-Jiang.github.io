## Algorithms & mathematical search {#algorithms}

<div class="ar-study-table-wrap ar-task-table" role="region" aria-label="Algorithmic and mathematical tasks" tabindex="0" markdown="1">

| Task name                 | Benchmark / source           | Description & eval time                                                                                                    | Saturation in recent work                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| :------------------------ | :--------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Polyomino packing         | Frontier-CS #0               | Minimize the packing rectangle. **Heavy: ~2–3 min** for 70 cases.                                                          | **Still improving.** [Test-Time Communication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21032) reports a new best-known result; [Fork-and-Flush](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.07447#S5) finds further search gains.                                                            |
| Job-shop scheduling       | Frontier-CS #46              | Minimize schedule makespan. **Light: ~20–40 s** for 15 cases.                                                              | **Unclear.** Recent [agent results](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.07447#S5) do not establish how close this exact suite is to the best scheduling methods.                                                                                                        |
| AHC014 / RectJoin         | AtCoder; Frontier-CS #159    | Add valid rectangles on a dot grid. **Heavy: ~1–5 min** for 30 cases; **~10–15 min** for 75.                               | **Unclear at the frontier.** [Fork-and-Flush](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.07447#S5) and [Forgetting](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.20563#page=6) improve agent baselines; case sets and budgets differ.                                                            |
| Toroidal string placement | AHC004; Frontier-CS #150     | Fit strings into a wrapping grid. **Heavy: ~15–20 min** for 100 cases.                                                     | **Unclear.** [Recent agent gains](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.07447#S5) are not a comparison against the strongest contest solutions under matched limits.                                                                                                      |
| Ice-rink routing          | Frontier-CS #171             | Visit targets using moves, slides and block edits. **Heavy: ~10–15 min** for 75 cases.                                     | **Unclear at the frontier.** [Forgetting](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.20563#page=6) and [Fork-and-Flush](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.07447#S5) improve baselines; 100 is a reference score, not a ceiling.                                                       |
| Sphere packing            | Frontier-CS #48              | Pack equal spheres into a cube. **Light: ~20–40 s** for 15 cases.                                                          | **Unclear for this suite.** [Small recent gains](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.07447#S5) do not establish saturation across its sphere counts.                                                                                                                    |
| Mobile relay layout       | Frontier-CS #307             | Place relay hubs and assign sensors. **Mixed: ~30–60 s** for 10 cases.                                                     | **Unclear.** The [72.73–72.82 cluster](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.07447#S5) is from one study; independent evidence of a shared ceiling is missing.                                                                                                            |
| Order-add                 | EdgeBench                    | Optimize a permutation through a cost oracle. **Light/Mixed:** ~seconds–minutes to score; solver runs can take hours–days. | **Unclear; check the grader.** An [independent audit](https://lishengzhi.com/edgebench-verified/) finds best-submission selection errors that can hide later improvements.                                                                                      |
| Circle packing, n = 26    | AlphaEvolve-style task; CCTS | Maximize summed radii. **Light** final check, ~sub-second–seconds; **Mixed** optimizer run, ~seconds–minutes.              | **Hard to improve meaningfully.** [TTT-Discover, ThetaEvolve and SimpleTES](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.19341) repeatedly reach ≈2.635983. Tiny differences require matched feasibility tolerances.                                                             |
| Arithmetic Kakeya         | CCTS mathematical suite      | Optimize an entropy-based construction. **Light** check, ~sub-second–seconds; **Mixed** search, ~seconds–minutes.          | **Diminishing gains at the frontier.** The matching [four-slope entropy record](https://teorth.github.io/optimizationproblems/constants/3c.html) improved in 2026, but the latest increments are around 10⁻⁹–10⁻¹¹.                                             |
| Heilbronn’s triangle      | CCTS mathematical suite      | Place 11 points to maximize the smallest triangle. **Light** check; **Mixed** search, ~seconds–minutes.                    | **Hard to improve at n = 11.** [MLEvolve and AlphaEvolve](https://github.com/InternScience/MLEvolve#mathematical-optimization-results) effectively tie at 0.03652989; the [Arena leaders](https://einsteinarena.com/problems/heilbronn-triangles) reproduce it. |
| Squares in a square       | CCTS mathematical suite      | Fit non-overlapping squares with maximum summed side length. **Light** check; **Mixed** search, ~seconds–minutes.          | **Unclear; depends on n.** [Recent theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22163) studies the same side-length objective, but does not establish a common empirical ceiling for CCTS’s task.                                                                         |
| Synthetic concept search  | CCTS controlled environment  | Mutate concept sets and sum their utilities. **Light: ~sub-second** scoring.                                               | **Not applicable.** The utility landscape is constructed by [CCTS](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.03132v1); it has no external research frontier.                                                                                                                  |

| Workload speedups                                            | SWE-fficiency-Lite              | Speed up repository workloads without breaking tests. **Heavy: 3–93 min**.                                                                   | **Still improving.** [PerfAgent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19653) raises expert-matching patches from 26% to 74%; [DuoDiscover](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17184#S3.SS1) reports further workload speedups.                    |
| JAX kernels                                                  | JAXBench                        | Optimize TPU kernels. **Heavy: 7–32 min**.                                                                                                   | **Still improving.** [JAXBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20466) leaves gaps to tuned Pallas kernels; [DuoDiscover](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17184#S3.SS1) reports newer gains. | Offloaded MoE inference                                      | FreeToken case study            | Optimize single-GPU inference with host-resident weights. **Heavy: 70 min**.                                                                 | **Unclear for this workload.** [FreeToken](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16157) spans many hardware/model settings; [DuoDiscover’s case](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17184#S3.SS2) does not define a common ceiling.                |
| Database execution                                           | SQLite case study               | Optimize engine performance with correctness checks. **Heavy: 10 min**.                                                                      | **Unclear for this workload.** [DuoDiscover’s SQLite gain](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17184#S3.SS2) is evidence of an opportunity, not a literature-wide saturation test.                                                       |
| Compression                                                  | zstd case study                 | Reduce instructions while preserving output bytes. **Heavy: 15–25 min**.                                                                     | **Unclear for this workload.** [DuoDiscover](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17184#S3.SS2) improves a byte-preserving zstd task; broader codec results optimize different objectives.                                                |
| CPU data prefetching                                         | DPC4 / ChampSim                 | Evaluate prefetcher designs across simulation traces. **Heavy: up to 28 h** on the study’s cluster.                                          | **Still improving, with costly gains.** [ArchAgent v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09874) beats the DPC4 champion by 0.3% overall; [DuoDiscover](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17184#S3.SS2) explores further designs.              |

</div>

DuoDiscover’s timings above are [reported full-evaluation measurements](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17184#A2) on its hardware. Its proxies are much lighter: median **16 s** on sampled SOL-Exec kernels and **40 s** on sampled SWE-fficiency tasks. These are different evaluators, not faster runs of the same full test.

<details class="ar-eval-notes" markdown="1">
<summary>How the estimates are formed</summary>

For the Frontier-CS suites, a useful budget is **number of cases × solver time per case**, plus compilation and checking. The public configs give [polyomino](https://github.com/FrontierCS/Frontier-CS/blob/main/algorithmic/problems/0/config.yaml) 70 × 2 s, [job-shop](https://github.com/FrontierCS/Frontier-CS/blob/main/algorithmic/problems/46/config.yaml) and [sphere packing](https://github.com/FrontierCS/Frontier-CS/blob/main/algorithmic/problems/48/config.yaml) 15 × 1 s, [relay layout](https://github.com/FrontierCS/Frontier-CS/blob/main/algorithmic/problems/307/config.yaml) 10 × 4 s, [AHC014](https://github.com/FrontierCS/Frontier-CS/blob/main/algorithmic/problems/159/config.yaml) and [p171](https://github.com/FrontierCS/Frontier-CS/blob/main/algorithmic/problems/171/config.yaml) 75 × 10 s, and [p150](https://github.com/FrontierCS/Frontier-CS/blob/main/algorithmic/problems/150/config.yaml) 100 × 10 s. These estimate a solver that uses most of its allowance, not an observed average. Faster solvers, fewer cases and parallel workers reduce wall time; the papers may use different subsets.

Other ranges are order-of-magnitude judgments from the task: direct numerical checks are cheap, while compilation, repeated test workloads, simulations and retraining add cost. Unbounded numerical search has no fixed duration. [MIRA’s task protocols](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02525#A2), for example, distinguish graph scoring from training each architecture afresh. Model-judged tasks include the evaluator’s LLM calls. A larger judge or a slower service can push them beyond the range shown.

</details>
