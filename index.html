Chonghe Jiang

PhD student · MIT IDSS

I am a PhD student at MIT IDSS, advised by Prof. Jinhua Zhao. I also work with Prof. Paul Liang.

I study how LLMs acquire and internalize knowledge as they improve themselves. My research connects two directions:

  • Learning and self-improvement. Reinforcement learning, LLM post-training, and test-time learning for reasoning and discovery.
  • Optimization and decision-making. Optimization theory and algorithms, and LLMs that can formulate optimization problems and design efficient algorithms.

Before MIT, I received my M.Phil in Operations Research from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, working with Prof. Anthony Man-Cho So and Prof. Viet Anh Nguyen. I received my bachelor’s degree in Mathematics from Tongji University in 2023.
