## Models & Tasks {#experiments}

Eval time covers one candidate’s execution and scoring, excluding LLM generation. **Light** means short checks; **Heavy** means minutes or longer. **NR** means the paper does not report the time; **†** marks a qualitative estimate from the workload. A cheap final check can still follow an expensive solver run.
