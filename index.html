Chonghe Jiang

PhD student · MIT IDSS

I am a PhD student at MIT IDSS with Prof. Jinhua Zhao. I also work with Prof. Paul Liang.

Before relocating to Cambridge, I spent two years in Hong Kong and received my M.Phil in Operations Research from the Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) under Prof. Anthony Man-Cho So and Prof. Viet Anh Nguyen. I obtained my bachelor’s degree in Mathematics (2019-2023) from Tongji University.

I now study how LLMs acquire and internalize knowledge as they improve themselves.

Research

Google Scholar ↗

I started my research career in 2022, studying optimization theory and game theory for complex decision-making systems (Goods markets, chores markets). Starting in 2024, motivated by the development of GenAI, I have explored the two-way interaction between optimization and LLMs: applying optimization methods to improve LLM training and inference (FLORAIN, GETS), and developing LLMs that can better perform optimization modeling (AlphaOPT) and algorithm design (FrontierOR). After joining MIT in 2025, I have become increasingly interested in the broader methodologies for improving LLMs themselves. I see LLMs as optimizers that can propose solutions, learn from feedback, and refine the approaches they generate (CORAL). My current research explores how test-time training can strengthen this self-improvement process, allowing models to improve their capabilities as they work on a problem (Guidance-TTT). I also spend time building REEF, infrastructure for continual learning.
