I am a PhD student at [MIT IDSS](https://idss.mit.edu/) with [Prof. Jinhua Zhao](https://zhaojinhua.com/). I also work with [Prof. Paul Liang](https://pliang279.github.io/).

Before relocating to Cambridge, I spent two years in Hong Kong and received my M.Phil in Operations Research from [the Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/) under [Prof. Anthony Man-Cho So](https://www1.se.cuhk.edu.hk/~manchoso/) and [Prof. Viet Anh Nguyen](https://www.vietanhnguyen.net/). I obtained my bachelor’s degree in Mathematics (2019-2023) from Tongji University.

I now study how **LLMs acquire and internalize knowledge as they improve themselves**.

<!-- research-background -->

I started my research career in 2022, studying optimization theory and game theory for complex decision-making systems. Starting in 2024, motivated by the development of GenAI, I have explored the two-way interaction between optimization and LLMs: applying optimization methods to improve LLM training and inference, and developing LLMs that can better perform optimization modeling and algorithm design. After joining MIT in 2025, I have become increasingly interested in the broader methodologies for improving LLMs themselves. I see LLMs as optimizers that can propose solutions, learn from feedback, and refine the approaches they generate. My current research explores how test-time training can strengthen this self-improvement process, allowing models to improve their capabilities as they work on a problem.
