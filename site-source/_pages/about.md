I am a PhD student at [MIT IDSS](https://idss.mit.edu/), advised by [Prof. Jinhua Zhao](https://zhaojinhua.com/). I also work with [Prof. Paul Liang](https://pliang279.github.io/).

I study how **LLMs acquire and internalize knowledge as they improve themselves**. My research connects two directions:

- **Learning and self-improvement.** Reinforcement learning, LLM post-training, and test-time learning for reasoning and discovery.
- **Optimization and decision-making.** Optimization theory and algorithms, and LLMs that can formulate optimization problems and design efficient algorithms.

Before MIT, I received my M.Phil in Operations Research from [The Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/), working with [Prof. Anthony Man-Cho So](https://www1.se.cuhk.edu.hk/~manchoso/) and [Prof. Viet Anh Nguyen](https://www.vietanhnguyen.net/). I received my bachelor’s degree in Mathematics from Tongji University in 2023.
