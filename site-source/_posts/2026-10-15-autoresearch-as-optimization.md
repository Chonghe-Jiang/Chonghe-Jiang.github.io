## Tasks {#tasks}

Eval time includes candidate execution and scoring, excluding the agent’s writing time. **~** marks estimates with warm environments, serial CPU cases and one GPU where needed. **Light** = seconds; **Heavy** = minutes or longer; **Mixed** spans both.

**Saturation** asks whether meaningful gains have become difficult across recent papers and public results. The judgments below reflect the literature as of **October 16, 2026**; a plateau in one run is not enough.

<details class="ar-eval-notes" markdown="1">
<summary>How the estimates are formed</summary>

For the Frontier-CS suites, a useful budget is **number of cases × solver time per case**, plus compilation and checking. The public configs give [polyomino](https://github.com/FrontierCS/Frontier-CS/blob/main/algorithmic/problems/0/config.yaml) 70 × 2 s, [job-shop](https://github.com/FrontierCS/Frontier-CS/blob/main/algorithmic/problems/46/config.yaml) and [sphere packing](https://github.com/FrontierCS/Frontier-CS/blob/main/algorithmic/problems/48/config.yaml) 15 × 1 s, [relay layout](https://github.com/FrontierCS/Frontier-CS/blob/main/algorithmic/problems/307/config.yaml) 10 × 4 s, [AHC014](https://github.com/FrontierCS/Frontier-CS/blob/main/algorithmic/problems/159/config.yaml) and [p171](https://github.com/FrontierCS/Frontier-CS/blob/main/algorithmic/problems/171/config.yaml) 75 × 10 s, and [p150](https://github.com/FrontierCS/Frontier-CS/blob/main/algorithmic/problems/150/config.yaml) 100 × 10 s. These estimate a solver that uses most of its allowance, not an observed average. Faster solvers, fewer cases and parallel workers reduce wall time; the papers may use different subsets.

Other ranges are order-of-magnitude judgments from the task: direct numerical checks are cheap, while compilation, repeated test workloads, simulations and retraining add cost. Unbounded numerical search has no fixed duration. [MIRA’s task protocols](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02525#A2), for example, distinguish graph scoring from training each architecture afresh. Model-judged tasks include the evaluator’s LLM calls. A larger judge or a slower service can push them beyond the range shown.

</details>
